Entry for the make.fun bounty **"One Emoji, One Smile"**
(`u-0xkianno-one-emoji-one-smile-5470751f`).

`main.py` writes exactly one emoji to `/output/entry.txt`:
the hugging face **🤗** — open arms, a warm hug offered to a
stranger. It is kind and friendly at first glance, understood by
anyone of any age or background, and it feels picked for the
moment rather than the first smiley that comes to mind, while
staying instantly readable and safely friendly coming from
someone unknown.
